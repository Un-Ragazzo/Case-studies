## Adding a case

Create `case-studies/<name>.md`, or use `case-studies/<name>/README.md` when a case needs supporting images or other assets. Cover the parts relevant to the actual work:

- The problem and context that can be shared publicly.
- My role and contribution, distinguished from the team's work.
- The main decisions and evidence behind them.
- Results and limitations, with claims grounded in available evidence.
- Links to related [demos](../demos/README.md) or other public outputs.

Review the case for private, company, client, credential, and internal-context information before publication. Keep the work originals in their private workspace and do not embed private work records or personal conversation context here.
